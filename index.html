World in Numbers

Open data, explained in charts

World in Numbers is a YouTube channel that turns open public statistics into short, clear, animated chart videos. Each video answers one question with fresh data and credits every source.

Watch on YouTube: @WorldInNumbersWeekly

About the World in Numbers uploader

The "World in Numbers uploader" is a private publishing tool used only by the channel owner. It is not offered to other users. It:

  • downloads open statistics from public data providers and calculates the figures shown in each video;
  • renders charts, synthetic narration and captions into a video;
  • uploads the video as private to the owner's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API;
  • after the owner manually approves it, makes the video public and shares the short version to the owner's own Instagram and TikTok accounts.

Narration is a synthetic (AI) voice and scripts are AI-assisted. A human reviews every video before it is published. The content is statistical reporting, not financial or investment advice.
