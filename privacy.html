World in Numbers

Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026

This policy covers the "World in Numbers uploader", a private publishing tool operated by the owner of the World in Numbers YouTube channel. The tool is used only by the channel owner to publish videos to the owner's own accounts. It is not offered to the public, and it does not create accounts for or collect data from other people.

Data the tool accesses

  • Google / YouTube: with the owner's permission, the tool uses the YouTube Data API scopes youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl. They are used only to upload videos, set thumbnails and captions, and change the privacy status of videos on the owner's own channel.
  • Instagram (Meta): the tool uses the owner's Page access token to publish short videos (Reels) to the owner's own Instagram professional account.
  • TikTok: the tool uses the user.info.basic and video.upload scopes to send short videos to the owner's own TikTok drafts. The owner finishes and publishes each post in the TikTok app.

The tool does not read, collect or store viewers' personal data, comments, messages, contacts or analytics.

How data is stored

The only stored data are the access and refresh tokens needed to publish. They are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's GitHub Actions configuration and are used only while a publishing job runs. Videos and their metadata are generated from public statistics and contain no personal data.

Sharing and use

Data obtained from Google, Meta or TikTok is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. It is used only to publish the owner's own videos.

World in Numbers' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time. For Google, use myaccount.google.com/permissions. For Meta, revoke access in the Business Integrations settings. For TikTok, use the app's Manage app permissions settings. Revoking access stops all publishing, and the stored tokens are then deleted from GitHub.

Viewers

When you watch World in Numbers videos on YouTube, Instagram or TikTok, those platforms' own privacy policies apply. World in Numbers receives no personal data about viewers beyond the aggregated statistics those platforms show to every creator.
